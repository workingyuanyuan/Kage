05 · MATERIAL

4 Layer Stacked Background Architecture

The background is four layers: a drawn base, a warm leak, an optional central sink, and grain that always sits on top. Arcs and dust are decorative motifs, not part of the four.

Flat digital pixels lack texture. We overlay a 200×200 grayscale PNG grain generated with a fixed seed, yielding a luma standard deviation of 7.4.

Light leaks are graded across 3 page levels: narrative pages cap at 24%/19%, while reading pages drop to 10%/8%. Light core stays outside, keeping center body luma under 20.

L1 · Drawn base
Pure black #000000. This is the colour actually painted, not the colour you see.
L2 · Warm leak
Screen blend. The core is anchored off-canvas; only the falloff enters the frame.
L3 · Central sink (optional)
Needed only over a full-bleed image. A flat base needs none — the centre is already darkest.
L4 · Film grain
Normal blend, always topmost. Composited luminance lands near 12.8.

06 · MOTIFS

08 · ACCEPTANCE

8 Quantifiable Automated Verification Checks

Build pipeline automatically verifies 8 physical visual metrics, ensuring all 16 template outputs match spec.

  1. 01
    1. Reverse Vignetting DistributionLuma of center 20% region must be lower than outer 10% edge region.
  2. 02
    2. Edge StabilityCross-screenshot edge luma variance must stay within ±10%.
  3. 03
    3. Luma Area RatioLuma ≥48 ratio < 4%, ≥80 < 0.15%, <32 ≥ 84%.
  4. 04
    4. Grain Standard DeviationOverlaid grain luma standard deviation must stay in 6–14, baseline at 7.4.
  5. 05
    5. Three-Channel Achromatic CheckRGB three-channel residual amplitude diff must be less than 5% to guarantee noise neutrality.
  6. 06
    6. Clean Region Low-Frequency ResidualClean area 64/128px scale residual must be less than 0.7 to prevent low-frequency mottling.
  7. 07
    7. Background Hue RangeBackground hue coordinates restricted strictly to 18–30° or 180–192°.
  8. 08
    8. Body Column Luma ConstraintBody column low-frequency luma p99 ≤ 20 and p99−p1 ≤ 8, guaranteeing contrast.

09 · QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About kage

Addressing 4 core questions regarding system positioning, scope, aesthetics, and develop mode.

1. What is kage?
kage is an AI Agent Skill for generating styled HTML documents. It is not a generic landing page builder, but a complete document design system driven by 16 templates and 45 color tokens.
2. What can kage generate?
Supports 8 types: one-pager, long-doc, equity-report, portfolio, letter, changelog, slides, and landing-page, totaling 16 pure screen HTML templates across Chinese and English tracks.
3. What is the core aesthetic philosophy?
The aesthetic derives from analog film stock and classic cinema physics. Through a #000000 base, 7.4 SD noise, and 18–30° light leak, digital sharpness is converted into cinematic depth.
4. What is Develop Mode?
Develop Mode is an objective physical observer perspective that translates abstract claims into observable records. It is a rarely used optional voice, disabled by default, requiring explicit user request.
